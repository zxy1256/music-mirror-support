Is Music Mirror a piano course?

No. It is a practice companion for organizing your own pieces, references, notes and recordings. It works alongside your existing lessons.

Can a teacher manage a whole studio?

Music Mirror is built for individual practice. Students can share recordings themselves; there is no teacher dashboard, remote assignment system or automatic studio sharing.

Where are notes and recordings stored?

Practice data stays on your device unless you choose to share it. No account is required. External content such as YouTube videos loads from its provider and follows that provider’s privacy practices. See our privacy policy.

Can I watch YouTube references offline?

YouTube links use the embedded YouTube player and require internet access. Music Mirror does not download or provide offline copies of YouTube videos.

Which devices can I use?

Download Music Mirror for iPhone or iPad with iOS 18.5 or iPadOS 18.5 or later. Check the App Store for current compatibility and availability.
